NP Membership

NP Membership is available for non-profit organizations with no membership fee.

  • No membership fee
  • Access to chamber events
  • Networking opportunities
  • Community participation

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership provides essential benefits for growing businesses.

  • Access to chamber events
  • Networking opportunities
  • Business training opportunities
  • Member discounts

Silver Membership

Silver Membership provides additional promotional and business benefits.

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Additional training opportunities
  • Event discounts
  • Business advertising opportunities

Gold Membership

Gold Membership provides premium benefits and greater visibility.

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Premium advertising opportunities
  • Home page spotlight opportunities
  • Priority event promotion
